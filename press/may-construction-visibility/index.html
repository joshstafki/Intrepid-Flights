MAY Focus on Construction: The Zimmerman HWY 169 S Underpass

The Highway 169 corridor through Zimmerman is currently undergoing a massive structural transformation. Converting this heavily traveled stretch into a true freeway system is a logistical heavy lift, involving complex grade changes, utility relocation, and the continuous management of local traffic flow.

For contractors, project managers, and safety teams operating on the ground, visibility is inherently limited. Earthworks, heavy machinery, and temporary barricades create blind spots that can slow down operations and complicate safety protocols. Ground-level sightlines can only tell part of the story.

This is where aerial data becomes critical infrastructure. Intrepid Flights documented the active build zone for the Highway 169 underpass project, giving teams an unobstructed, comprehensive view of the site. By capturing access points, material staging areas, and surrounding grade work from above, drone imagery eliminates the guesswork.

Having a 360-degree top-down perspective allows site managers to track daily progress against engineering plans, verify that safety perimeters are established correctly, and allocate resources without needing to physically traverse hazardous terrain. When the stakes are high and the project footprint is vast, reliable aerial intelligence keeps crews informed and major infrastructure projects moving forward safely.
