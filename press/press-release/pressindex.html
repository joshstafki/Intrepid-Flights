Intrepid Flights Unveils Digital Press Index for Global Media Access

Feb. 23, 2026 — Intrepid Flights is proud to announce the official launch of its comprehensive Press Index, a centralized digital hub designed to streamline communication with journalists, industry analysts, and the traveling public.

As Intrepid Flights continues to expand its global footprint, the need for a transparent, real-time repository of company news became paramount. The newly launched Index provides instant access to high-resolution media assets, executive biographies, and an archived history of corporate milestones.

The Intrepid Flights Press Index is now live and fully accessible to the public at:
intrepidflights.com/press

"Transparency and accessibility are at the heart of our mission," said the Intrepid Flights Communications Team. "By moving our resources to a centralized index, we are ensuring that our story—and the future of modern aviation—is available to everyone at the click of a button."
