Intrepid Flights

Operational Update: Enhanced Screening and Temporary Service Policies

Location: Hanover, MN
Effective: Immediately

In light of evolving global security conditions and heightened national considerations, Intrepid Flights is implementing a series of temporary operational refinements to ensure the highest standards of safety and regulatory compliance.

Current geopolitical shifts have increased the importance of rigorous oversight for aerial technologies. To remain a leader in responsible operations, we are enacting the following adjustments:

Enhanced Client Vetting Implementation of rigorous project and identity screening for all new and existing partnerships.
Service Radius Adjustment Strategic focus on operations within the primary Twin Cities service zone.
Booking Windows Standard 48-hour minimum window for comprehensive operational planning.
Priority Scheduling Preference allocated to repeat and commercial accounts.
Weather Enforcement Strict adherence to safety-related rescheduling to maintain flight integrity.

These measures are temporary and will be reassessed as international conditions stabilize. Our commitment to high-quality, compliant aerial service remains unwavering.

For further inquiries or detailed documentation, please contact our media desk at info@intrepidflights.com.
