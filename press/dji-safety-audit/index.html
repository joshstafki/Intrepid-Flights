From DJI: DJI Releases Findings of the Most Comprehensive Independent Security Assessment of Its Drone Systems to Date

A definitive referendum assuring Intrepid Flights customers of the safety and findings of DJI safety audits regarding data privacy and platform security.

Minnesota — At Intrepid Flights, protecting client data and maintaining operational security is foundational to everything we do. As a premier provider of drone photography and mapping services in the Twin Cities, we rely on industry-leading equipment to deliver precision results. Today, we are sharing important findings to continue assuring Intrepid Flights customers of the safety and findings of DJI safety audits regarding the platforms utilized for our aerial capture.

On May 28, 2026, DJI released the results of an independent security assessment conducted by OnDefend, a trusted U.S.-based cybersecurity firm. This rigorous assessment evaluated prominent systems, including the DJI Air 3S and the DJI Matrice 4E, testing them comprehensively across software, hardware, and radio frequency vulnerabilities.

The results decisively validate the security of the platforms we operate. According to the audit, no evidence was found of data transmission outside the United States, and all observed connections from DJI flight control applications were successfully routed to U.S.-based infrastructure. Furthermore, OnDefend identified no backdoors, no unauthorized remote access mechanisms, and no evidence of supply chain tampering or unapproved hardware modifications.

For our partners—from commercial real estate developers to infrastructure managers—these findings serve as a referendum confirming that sensitive aerial imagery, operational data, and mapping materials remain securely contained and protected. You can trust that our technology stack has been independently verified to the highest modern security standards.

To read the complete findings and learn more about the scope of the cybersecurity audit, please visit the official announcement: From DJI: DJI Releases Findings of the Most Comprehensive Independent Security Assessment of Its Drone Systems to Date.
