Spring 2026
Photography Campaign

Miami • Minneapolis • April – May 2026

Miami, FL April 24 – 26, 2026

Intrepid Flights  /  Flight Operations  |  April 20, 2026

INTREPID FLIGHTS — Intrepid Flights is proud to announce the official launch of its Spring 2026 Photography Campaign, beginning this weekend in Miami, Florida, April 24–26, 2026. The campaign marks the brand's first major operational deployment of the year and sets the tone for a season of elevated visual storytelling from the skies.

The Spring Campaign opens along the Miami coastline — a location chosen for its dynamic interplay of urban architecture, Atlantic light, and aerial perspective. The three-day Miami shoot will serve as the visual cornerstone of the season, producing flagship imagery across Intrepid Flights' full portfolio of aerial photography work.

Following the Miami deployment, Intrepid Flights returns to its home base in Minneapolis, Minnesota, where the full spring campaign will continue through April and May. The combined arc of Miami and Minneapolis represents the brand's keystone spring season — a deliberate, two-market push that anchors the first half of the 2026 operational calendar.

Spring 2026 represents a significant moment for Intrepid Flights. April and May are historically the brand's most fertile months for aerial composition, offering the widest variance in landscape, weather, and natural light across both the Sunbelt and the Upper Midwest. The dual-city campaign is structured to leverage both environments in sequence, producing a body of work that spans geography and season.

Work from the Spring 2026 campaign will be featured across Intrepid Flights' platforms. Follow the campaign at intrepidflights.com and across social channels for real-time updates, behind-the-scenes coverage, and published imagery from both the Miami and Minneapolis deployments.

For media inquiries, licensing, or collaboration opportunities, contact Intrepid Flights through the official website.
