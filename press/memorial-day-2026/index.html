News Release For Immediate Release — May 26, 2026

On Memorial Day,
We Fly With Purpose.

Intrepid Flights pauses to honor those who gave everything — and to reflect on what it means to look up at the sky in freedom.

Intrepid Flights — Official Statement Minneapolis–Saint Paul, MN

Every time we launch a drone, we do it beneath skies secured by sacrifice. This Memorial Day, Intrepid Flights takes a moment to set down the cameras and look up — not through a lens, but with gratitude.

Memorial Day is not a marketing occasion. It is a day of reckoning — a national pause to remember the men and women of the United States Armed Forces who did not come home. Their names are carved in stone and stitched into flags. Their service made possible every ordinary thing we do in freedom, including the simple act of flying.

At Intrepid Flights, aerial imaging is our craft — capturing landscapes, construction sites, properties, and events from perspectives that inspire. But perspective is also something we try to carry in how we operate. On days like this, the work stops. The gratitude doesn't.

We encourage our clients, partners, and the broader Twin Cities community to take time today to visit a local memorial, reach out to a Gold Star family, or simply stand outside and look up in silence. If you know a veteran or active service member, let them know they are seen.

Intrepid Flights will resume standard operations on Tuesday, May 27, 2026. We look forward to continuing to serve our clients with the same commitment to quality and professionalism that those we honor today brought to their own service.

About Intrepid Flights

Intrepid Flights is a licensed, FAA Part 107–compliant aerial imaging company based in the Minneapolis–Saint Paul metropolitan area. Founded and operated by Josh Stafki, Intrepid Flights provides professional drone photography and videography services for real estate, construction, events, and commercial applications across the Twin Cities region.
