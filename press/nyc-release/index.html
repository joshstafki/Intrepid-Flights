Intrepid Flights is Coming to New York City

The Twin Cities–based aerial imaging and drone photography studio announces an upcoming presence in New York City this spring.

Minnesota — Intrepid Flights, the Twin Cities–based aerial imaging and drone photography studio, announces an upcoming presence in New York City this spring.

Dates on Location: May 29 – June 3, 2026

Further details regarding availability and bookings will be shared in the days ahead. Inquiries are welcome in the meantime.

About Intrepid Flights

Intrepid Flights is a Minneapolis–Saint Paul aerial photography and imaging company serving commercial, editorial, and real estate clients across the Twin Cities metro. Founded and operated by FAA certified pilot Josh Stafki, Intrepid Flights delivers precision aerial content for clients who demand quality and reliability above the skyline.
