    <article class="article-body">
      <p><strong>Minnesota</strong> — Intrepid Flights is pleased to announce the formal launch of its Digital Media Licensure Program — a structured offering that grants individuals, businesses, and creative professionals access to our growing library of high-definition drone photography and aerial imaging content.</p>

      <p>Since our founding, Intrepid Flights has built an extensive catalog of aerial imagery spanning landscapes, infrastructure, waterways, urban environments, and seasonal conditions across the Twin Cities metropolitan area and beyond. With this program, that library is now available for commercial and editorial licensing.</p>

      <p>License holders will gain access to <strong>over 10,000 HD drone images</strong>, all delivered at full resolution and supported by flexible license tiers. Each file is cleared for use under the terms of the selected agreement. Whether the need is for a single-use editorial placement or a broad commercial campaign, our program is structured to accommodate projects of any scale.</p>

      <p>This launch reflects our commitment to extending the value of aerial perspective beyond project-specific commissions — making professional imagery more accessible while upholding the quality standards our clients have come to expect.</p>

      <p>Additional details regarding license tiers, pricing, subject categories, and access procedures will be published at intrepidflights.com in the coming weeks. Inquiries may be directed through our official contact channels.</p>

      <p>We look forward to supporting your next project from above.</p>
