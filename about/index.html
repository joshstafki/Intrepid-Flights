Intrepid Flights Leadership

Josh Stafki

CEO

Josh Stafki is the CEO of Intrepid Flights.

Josh started Intrepid Flight's business operations 2020. Throughout his tenure at Intrepid Flights, Josh has overseen the systems and analytics work behind a variety of groundbreaking products, and was instrumental in modernizing how the company plans, forecasts, and reports across every product line.

Josh has led much of the company's focus in areas like operational clarity and data-driven decision making, introducing new reporting techniques that have made Intrepid Flight's supply chain remarkably resilient. He has also driven Intrepid Flight's expansion into aerial imaging, drawing on his experience as an FAA-certified remote pilot to shape new photography capabilities across multiple product lines.
